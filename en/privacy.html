ACSO Consulting EOOD, a company incorporated under Bulgarian law with its registered office in Sofia, operates the website acsoconsulting.com and is the controller of the personal data described in this notice.

Full registration details are provided on first contact. To write to us, use the contact form.

2. What data we collect

Data reaches us by two routes only:

a) Data you enter in the contact form

  • Name
  • Business e-mail address
  • Company
  • What you build
  • Country in which your company is registered
  • Your current presence in Bulgaria
  • Telephone number (optional)
  • Free text about your enquiry (optional)

You submit this data voluntarily. It is used solely to answer your enquiry and to assess whether our activities match what you need.

b) Analytics cookies (only with your consent)

We use Google Analytics 4 (GA4) and Microsoft Clarity to understand how visitors use the site. These tools collect anonymised usage data such as pages viewed, scroll depth, click patterns and referral source. Clarity additionally produces anonymised session recordings and heatmaps. Sensitive form inputs are masked automatically.

3. Legal basis for processing

  • Contact form data: legitimate interest (Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR), in order to follow up on your request for information about our activities.
  • Analytics cookies: consent (Art. 6(1)(a) GDPR), only after explicit consent given through the banner.

4. How we use your data

  • To answer your enquiry within one working day
  • To assess whether our activities are relevant to you
  • To understand aggregated usage patterns (analytics, subject to consent)

Your data is not used for automated decision-making, profiling or advertising.

5. Who receives your data

  • Google LLC (processor for analytics, under EU standard contractual clauses), only where cookie consent was given
  • Microsoft Corporation (Clarity session analytics, under EU standard contractual clauses), only where cookie consent was given
  • Hosting provider: our web server processes form submissions; no data beyond what is needed to forward the message by e-mail is transmitted

We do not sell, rent or pass on your personal data to any other third party.

6. Retention period

  • Contact form data: up to 12 months after the last communication, then deleted.
  • Analytics data: up to 14 months in Google Analytics, then deleted automatically.

7. Your rights

Under the GDPR you have the right to:

  • obtain access to the data concerning you,
  • request correction of inaccurate data,
  • request erasure of your data,
  • withdraw consent at any time (by deleting the cookies or choosing “Reject” in the banner),
  • lodge a complaint with the competent supervisory authority. In Bulgaria this is the Commission for Personal Data Protection (KZLD); you may also complain to the supervisory authority of your own country of residence.

To exercise these rights, use the contact form. We respond within 30 days.

8. Data security

Your data is protected by HTTPS encryption in transit, server-side validation, submission rate limiting and restricted access to form submissions. No system is entirely secure, but the measures applied are proportionate to the sensitivity of the data.

9. Changes to this notice

This notice may be updated from time to time. The “last updated” date at the top indicates the current version. Material changes are announced on the site.
