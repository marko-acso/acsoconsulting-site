2. What data we collect

Data reaches us by three routes:

a) Data you enter in the contact form

  • Name
  • Business e-mail address
  • Company

  • Country in which your company is registered

You submit this data voluntarily. It is used solely to answer your enquiry and to assess whether our activities match what you need.

b) Analytics cookies (only with your consent)

We use Google Analytics 4 (GA4) and Microsoft Clarity to understand how visitors use the site. These tools collect usage data such as pages viewed, scroll depth, click patterns and referral source. Clarity additionally produces session recordings and heatmaps. Sensitive form inputs are masked automatically.

This data is pseudonymised: it is processed under a cookie identifier that does not name you directly. It therefore remains personal data within the meaning of Art. 4(1) GDPR and is not anonymous. Your IP address is also processed by these services; we do not truncate or mask it.

c) Your IP address

When you submit the contact form, our server derives an MD5 digest from your IP address and stores the timestamps of your submissions under that digest, in order to enforce a limit of five submissions per IP address per hour. The IP address itself is not stored in clear text. Separately, the web server keeps ordinary access logs which contain the IP address; these serve the secure operation of the service and the investigation of faults and attacks.

3. Legal basis for processing

  • Contact form data: legitimate interest (Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR), in order to follow up on your request for information about our activities.
  • Analytics cookies: consent (Art. 6(1)(a) GDPR), only after explicit consent given through the banner.
  • IP address (abuse prevention, rate limiting, server logs): legitimate interest (Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR). Our interest is to prevent automated abuse of the contact form and to keep the service secure and available.

4. How we use your data

  • To understand aggregated usage patterns (analytics, subject to consent)

Your data is not used for automated decision-making, profiling or advertising.

6. Retention period

  • Contact form data: up to 12 months after the last communication, then deleted.
  • Analytics data: up to 14 months in Google Analytics, then deleted automatically.
  • Rate limiting (MD5 digest of the IP address): the stored timestamps are evaluated over a rolling period of one hour; older entries are discarded on the next submission.
  • Web server access logs: overwritten automatically after the period configured by the hosting provider. We use them only to analyse faults and attacks, never to evaluate your behaviour.

7. Your rights

Under the GDPR you have the right to:

  • obtain access to the data concerning you,
  • request correction of inaccurate data,
  • request erasure of your data,
  • withdraw consent at any time,
  • lodge a complaint with the competent supervisory authority. In Bulgaria this is the Commission for Personal Data Protection (KZLD); you may also complain to the supervisory authority of your own country of residence.

Withdrawal of consent (Art. 7(3) GDPR). You may withdraw your consent to analytics cookies at any time. Withdrawal does not affect the lawfulness of processing carried out on the basis of that consent before it was withdrawn. To withdraw, use the “Withdraw cookie consent” control at the foot of this page, or the “Cookies” control in the site footer. This resets the stored consent, switches analytics off and deletes the analytics cookies already set; the consent banner then appears again.

To exercise these rights, use the contact form. We respond within 30 days.

8. Data security

Your data is protected by HTTPS encryption in transit, server-side validation, submission rate limiting and restricted access to form submissions. No system is entirely secure, but the measures applied are proportionate to the sensitivity of the data.
